# LOCAL-SEO-CHECKLIST.md — Meraki Weddings

## Google Business Profile strategy

**Recommendation: do not create a second, separate Google Business Profile for Meraki Weddings.**
Google's guidelines are strict about one GBP per physical location/business, and a wedding
service line operating from the same address/team as Meraki Audio is not a separate location.
Two profiles risk suspension for both.

Instead:
1. Add "Wedding ceremony audio" (or the closest matching category available) as an **additional
   service/category** on the existing Meraki Audio Google Business Profile.
2. Add `weddings.merakiaud.com` as an additional link/description mention on the existing profile
   where GBP allows secondary URLs.
3. If demand clearly justifies it later, revisit a Service Area Business profile specifically for
   weddings — but only after this subdomain has independent traction, not at launch.

## Bing Places

Mirror the same approach: update the existing Meraki Audio Bing Places listing with the wedding
service line and a link to the subdomain, rather than creating a second listing.

## Apple Business Connect

Same logic — one listing, updated to reflect the wedding service as part of Meraki Audio's
offerings, with `weddings.merakiaud.com` referenced as a secondary link where the platform
supports it.

## Wedding directories

Once the business owner confirms pricing/package details (see `CONTENT-QUESTIONS.md`), consider
listing on:
- The Knot
- WeddingWire
- Zola (vendor directory)

List as an **audio/sound vendor** category specifically, not "DJ" — this matters for both search
placement and for setting correct expectations with couples browsing the directory.

## Venue and planner partnerships

- Reach out to Tri-Cities-area wedding venues that do **not** provide their own ceremony sound —
  these are the highest-intent partners, since they already field "do you have a mic system?"
  questions from couples.
- Wedding planners are a strong referral source specifically for couples who are skipping a DJ
  but still need ceremony audio — this is a distinct-enough need that planners often don't have a
  default vendor for it.
- Ask each partner venue/planner for a **link from their own website** to
  `weddings.merakiaud.com` — this is a genuine local backlink, not a paid placement, and it
  reinforces local relevance to Google.

## Photographer partnerships

Wedding photographers are frequently on-site for the exact moments ceremony audio affects (vows,
processional). A simple cross-referral relationship (photographer mentions Meraki Weddings to
couples asking about ceremony logistics; Meraki mentions trusted photographers) is low-cost and
high-relevance.

## Review collection

- Do not display any reviews or ratings on the site until real ones exist — no fabricated
  testimonials or ratings were added anywhere in this build (see `CONTENT-QUESTIONS.md`).
- Once weddings have been completed under this service line, collect reviews on Google Business
  Profile directly (easiest to manage under the single existing profile) and optionally on
  wedding-directory listings.

## Local citations

Keep NAP (name/address/phone) consistent everywhere Meraki Audio / Meraki Weddings is listed —
citation consistency is a real local-ranking factor. Since no public phone number is confirmed
yet for the wedding service specifically (see `CONTENT-QUESTIONS.md`), resolve that before
building out citations that reference it.
